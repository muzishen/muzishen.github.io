About Me

Hi 😄! I am currently a Research Fellow at the NExT++ Center at the National University of Singapore (NUS). I actively collaborate with leading industry partners such as Tencent AI Lab, Huawei, AIUNI, GuijiAI, and Mobvoi, focusing on cutting-edge research in 2D/3D content generation. My research interests lie in human-centered, controllable, and consistent visual generation, including pose-guided person generation, virtual try-on, and long-term talking face synthesis, particularly in the context of e-commerce applications. Motivated by the vision of achieving digital immortality and machine consciousness, I am dedicated to pushing the boundaries of generative AI. Prior to my current position, I was an avid algorithm competition enthusiast, securing over 50 top-three finishes, including five championships in CCF A-class workshop challenges. I am a strong advocate of sharing🌱, collaborating🤝, advancing🚀, and innovating💡. Passionate about bridging fundamental research with real-world impact, I strive to ensure my work contributes to both academic progress and practical value. I am also actively seeking industrial collaborations and exploring potential entrepreneurial opportunities. If you’re interested in collaborating or would like to connect, feel free to reach out via email! 😊
